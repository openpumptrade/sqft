          How it works

          How it works

          Two machines share this site. The engine turns fees into square feet. The survey crew is a browser pool that will split SOL pots by proof of work.

          The engine

          01Claim

          The engine wallet claims the coin's creator rewards from pump.fun. SOL, on chain.

          02Convert

          Everything above a gas reserve is swapped on Solana through Jupiter into USDC, the only collateral the Parcl bridge accepts. One transaction, the signature is the receipt.

          03Bridge

          The USDC is deposited into the Parcl bridge program on Solana (minimum $5 per deposit). The deposit is credited to the Parcl account owned by the very same key, so nothing is custodied by a third party in between.

          04Open positions

          Free collateral becomes long positions on the city indices in the target book, cheapest deficit first, with IOC market orders at 1x: notional equals collateral, no borrowing. One unit of a market is one square foot of its index.

          05Hold

          Parcl Labs publishes each index once a day (7:50 AM ET). Positions are marked to that print; the only carry is the hourly funding rate. Square feet never get sold by the engine.

          06Repeat

          Every 10 minutes.

          The survey crew

          01Ticket

          Your tab asks the server for a plot bound to your address: a random 32-byte seed and a grid size in bits. Tickets last two minutes.

          02Walk the grid

          Web Workers on your CPU and a WebGPU compute shader on your GPU hash seed ‖ counter, looking for a point whose hash starts with grid size zero bits.

          03Plant a stake

          A hit is sent as its counter. The server checks it with a single hash. Weak, reused, expired or borrowed stakes are rejected.

          04Credit

          A stake at grid size d is credited 2d points, the number of points it takes on average to find one.

          05Grid size

          Each address gets its own grid size, tuned for about one stake every few seconds, so a phone and a workstation are measured the same way.

          06Round

          At round close the pot is split by share of verified survey work. Balances over the threshold are paid out and logged in the ledger.
